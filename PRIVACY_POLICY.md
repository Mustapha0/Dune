## Data processed on your device

DUNE requests Android Usage Access so it can estimate how long launchable apps have gone without being opened. DUNE stores the app labels/package names it can access, estimated inactivity values, archive/rescue state, and cleaning progress in a local Room database. Settings such as sound, haptics, and reminder preferences are stored locally.

DUNE does not include an internet permission, account system, cloud sync, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK in this project. The app list and local settings are not uploaded by DUNE. Android itself and other installed apps remain subject to their own privacy practices.

## Permissions

- **Usage Access:** used to estimate app inactivity and refresh the forgotten-app list.
- **Notifications (optional):** used for local reminders when enabled in DUNE settings.
- **Vibration (optional):** used for haptic feedback when enabled/available.

Usage Access and notification permission can be revoked through Android settings. If Usage Access is denied, Preview mode remains available but real inactivity estimates cannot be refreshed.

## Data retention and deletion

App records and preferences remain on the device until app data is cleared or DUNE is uninstalled. Android backup is disabled in this project configuration. Archived apps are only hidden inside DUNE; they are not uninstalled.
